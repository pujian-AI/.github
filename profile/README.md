# PUJIAN — sheet music recognition (OMR)

**PUJIAN** turns a photo, scan or PDF of printed sheet music into an editable digital score.

👉 **Try it in your browser: [seqnote.ink/en](https://seqnote.ink/en)**

## What it does

- **Photo / PDF → MusicXML & MIDI** — upload a phone photo or a PDF page, then download MusicXML (opens in MuseScore, Finale, Sibelius, Dorico) or MIDI.
- **Single-line and multi-part scores** — solo parts through ensemble scores, including alto and tenor clefs.
- **Listen and check** — play back the result in the browser and fix mistakes in the online editor.
- **Split parts** — pull a single part out of a full score for practice.
- **Transpose** — change key for a different instrument or voice.
